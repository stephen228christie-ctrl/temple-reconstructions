How it works

Faithful to the text, easy to explore

Every part is sourced

Each object is tied to its verses, quoted in full, with its size in cubits and metres. Each one is marked EXPLICIT IN TEXT or INTERPRETED, with the alternatives given.

On a laptop

W A S D to walk, the mouse to look, E to read. A gamepad works too. Graphics adjust to your machine.

On a phone

Left thumb to walk, right thumb to look, tap anything to read. The verses open in a sheet you can pull up. Works in portrait or landscape.

Picks up where you left off

Your place, views and settings are kept in your browser, so you can come back to the same spot.
